### English Version (First Appeal - Logo Issue)

Dear ARR Editors,

Thank you for your time and effort in managing our submission (Submission #2522). We are writing regarding the recent desk rejection of our paper. Specifically, we would like to clarify the formatting concern raised by the Program Chair on OpenReview (https://openreview.net/forum?id=N8IPkUnl4m) stating: "The .sty file has been modified. The title contains a logo."

We have strictly maintained the integrity of the official ACL style files. The hash of the `.sty` file used in our submission is identical to the official version, and the logo was simply inserted using the `graphicx` package within `main.tex`. As stated in the ARR Call for Papers (https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp), the core formatting requirement is that "Paper submissions must use the official ACL style template... Authors may not modify these style files or use template designed for other conferences." Our submission fully complies with this guideline, as no official style files were altered.

Furthermore, we note that including emojis or logos in the title is not uncommon in previous ACL papers, provided that the official templates remain unaltered. For instance, multiple ACL and EMNLP proceedings, such as https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1 and https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1200.pdf, have adopted this exact formatting. 

We deeply appreciate your understanding and consideration of this clarification.

Sincerely,
Authors of Submission #2522
